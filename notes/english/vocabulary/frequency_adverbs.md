# Frequency Adverbs

## Usage
Adverbs such as `always`, `usually`, `often`, `sometimes`, `rarely` and `never` normally come before a main verb: `She often studies at night`. They normally come after the verb `to be`: `She is often tired`.

Expressions such as `every day`, `once a week` and `on weekends` usually come at the beginning or end of the sentence.
