# There Is and There Are

## Grammar
Use `there is` with one thing or an uncountable noun, and `there are` with plural nouns. Use `there was` and `there were` for the past.

For negatives, add `not` after the verb. For questions, place the verb before `there`: `Is there a pharmacy nearby?` and `Are there any questions?`
