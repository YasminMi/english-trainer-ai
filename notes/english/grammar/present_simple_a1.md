# Present Simple A1

## Grammar
Use the present simple for routines, habits and facts. With I, you, we and they, use the base verb. With he, she and it, normally add `s`, `es` or change consonant + `y` to `ies`.

For negatives, use `do not` or `don't` with I, you, we and they. Use `does not` or `doesn't` with he, she and it. The main verb stays in the base form after `do`, `does`, `don't` or `doesn't`: `She doesn't like coffee`, not `She don't like coffee` and not `She doesn't likes coffee`. `Doesn't` is the negative auxiliary; it is not a negative form of the main verb `like`.

For questions, use `Do` or `Does` before the subject, followed by the base verb: `Does she work here?`


## Sentences
- en: I work every day.
  pt: Eu trabalho todo dia.
  tags: present_simple, affirmative

- en: She works in an office.
  pt: Ela trabalha em um escritório.
  tags: present_simple, affirmative

- en: They live in São Paulo.
  pt: Eles moram em São Paulo.
  tags: present_simple, affirmative

- en: He studies English at home.
  pt: Ele estuda inglês em casa.
  tags: present_simple, affirmative

- en: I do not work on Sundays.
  pt: Eu não trabalho aos domingos.
  tags: present_simple, negative

- en: She does not like coffee.
  pt: Ela não gosta de café.
  tags: present_simple, negative

- en: They do not speak English.
  pt: Eles não falam inglês.
  tags: present_simple, negative

- en: Do you work here?
  pt: Você trabalha aqui?
  tags: present_simple, question

- en: Does she speak Portuguese?
  pt: Ela fala português?
  tags: present_simple, question

- en: What do you do?
  pt: O que você faz?
  tags: present_simple, question

- en: Where do you work?
  pt: Onde você trabalha?
  tags: present_simple, question

- en: I go to the gym after work.
  pt: Eu vou à academia depois do trabalho.
  tags: present_simple, routine

- en: He drinks water every morning.
  pt: Ele bebe água toda manhã.
  tags: present_simple, routine

- en: We eat lunch at noon.
  pt: Nós almoçamos ao meio-dia.
  tags: present_simple, routine

## Flashcards
- work = trabalhar / trabalho
- study = estudar / estudo
- live = morar / viver
- speak = falar
- like = gostar
- drink = beber
- eat = comer
- go = ir

## Dictionary
- office = escritório
- Sunday = domingo
- noon = meio-dia
- gym = academia
- after = depois
