# Can and Can't

## Grammar
Use `can` for ability, possibility and informal permission. Use `can't` or `cannot` for the negative. A base verb comes directly after `can` or `can't`, without `to` and without an extra `s`: `She can swim`, not `She can swims`.

For questions, place `can` before the subject: `Can you help me?`


## Sentences
- en: I can swim.
  pt: Eu sei nadar.
  tags: can, ability

- en: She can speak English.
  pt: Ela sabe falar inglês.
  tags: can, ability

- en: We can help you.
  pt: Nós podemos te ajudar.
  tags: can, possibility

- en: He cannot drive.
  pt: Ele não sabe dirigir.
  tags: can, negative

- en: They cannot come today.
  pt: Eles não podem vir hoje.
  tags: can, negative

- en: Can you open the door?
  pt: Você pode abrir a porta?
  tags: can, question

- en: Can she play the guitar?
  pt: Ela sabe tocar violão?
  tags: can, question

- en: I can cook dinner.
  pt: Eu consigo fazer o jantar.
  tags: can, ability
