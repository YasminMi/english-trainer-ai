# To Be A1

## Grammar
In the present, use `am` with I, `is` with he, she and it, and `are` with you, we and they. In the past, use `was` with I, he, she and it, and `were` with you, we and they.

Add `not` after the verb for negatives. For questions, place the verb before the subject: `Is she ready?` and `Were they at home?`
