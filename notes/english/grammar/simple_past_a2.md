# Simple Past A2

## Grammar
Use the simple past for completed actions at a finished time. Regular verbs normally end in `ed`; irregular verbs have their own past forms.

For negatives, use `did not` or `didn't` followed by the base verb. For questions, use `Did`, the subject and the base verb: `Did she go?` The verb does not stay in the past form after `did` or `didn't`.


## Sentences
- en: I worked yesterday.
  pt: Eu trabalhei ontem.
  tags: past, regular

- en: She studied English last night.
  pt: Ela estudou inglês ontem à noite.
  tags: past, regular

- en: We watched a movie on Saturday.
  pt: Nós assistimos a um filme no sábado.
  tags: past, regular

- en: He did his homework.
  pt: Ele fez a lição de casa.
  tags: past, irregular

- en: They went to the beach.
  pt: Eles foram para a praia.
  tags: past, irregular

- en: I did not see your message.
  pt: Eu não vi sua mensagem.
  tags: past, negative

- en: She did not go to work.
  pt: Ela não foi para o trabalho.
  tags: past, negative

- en: Did you call your mother?
  pt: Você ligou para sua mãe?
  tags: past, question
