# Present Continuous A2

## Grammar
Use the present continuous for actions happening now or around the present. Form it with `am`, `is` or `are` followed by a verb ending in `ing`: `I am studying` and `They are working`.

Put `not` after the verb `to be` for negatives. For questions, move `am`, `is` or `are` before the subject. Stative verbs such as `know`, `believe` and `need` are not normally used in the continuous form.


## Sentences
- en: I am studying English now.

  tags: present_continuous, affirmative

- en: She is working from home today.

  pt: Ele está almoçando.
  tags: present_continuous, affirmative

- en: We are learning a lot.
  pt: Nós estamos aprendendo muito.

- en: I am not watching TV.
  pt: Eu não estou assistindo TV.

  pt: Ela não está dormindo.
  tags: present_continuous, negative

- en: Are you working now?
  pt: Você está trabalhando agora?

- en: Is he coming to the meeting?
  pt: Ele está vindo para a reunião?
  tags: present_continuous, question

- en: What are you doing?
  pt: O que você está fazendo?

- en: I am reading a good book.
  pt: Eu estou lendo um livro bom.
  tags: present_continuous, affirmative

- en: She is drinking coffee and working.
  pt: Ela está bebendo café e trabalhando.
